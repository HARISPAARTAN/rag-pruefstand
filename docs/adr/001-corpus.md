# ADR-001: Corpus of five federal energy laws

Status: accepted, 2026-10-25

Context: the bench needs German text that is free to redistribute, hard for retrieval and stable enough to freeze.

Decision: EnWG, EEG 2023, MsbG, EnFG and KWKG 2025 from gesetze-im-internet.de, downloaded once as XML, frozen by SHA-256,
parsed into one unit per Absatz. Laws are not protected by copyright (§ 5 UrhG); five laws are an insubstantial extract of
the database. The Munich citizen-service corpus (K2) stays a planned extension until its licence is confirmed.

Consequences: legal German with compounds, long definition lists and cross-references; the energy domain links to the
author's other projects. Results hold for this legal register, not for German text in general.
